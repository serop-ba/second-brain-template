# Log

Append-only record of everything that happens here. Newest entries at the
bottom. Never edit a past entry.

Entry format: `## [YYYY-MM-DD] type | Title`, where `type` is one of
`init`, `capture`, `ingest`, `weekly`, `report`, `decision`, `experiment`,
`lint`.

For an `experiment` entry, prefix the title with the unit it belongs to —
`## [2026-09-18] experiment | marketing/ads | Static creative round 1 — launched`.

Quick recall: `grep "^## \[" log.md | tail -5`
