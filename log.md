# Log

Append-only record of everything that happens here. Newest entries at the
bottom. Never edit a past entry.

Entry format: `## [YYYY-MM-DD] type | Title`, where `type` is one of
`init`, `capture`, `ingest`, `weekly`, `report`, `decision`, `video-idea`, `lint`.

Quick recall: `grep "^## \[" log.md | tail -5`
