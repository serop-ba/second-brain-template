# Content — experiments

Append-only. Newest at the bottom. **Never edit a past entry** — except to fill
in `Result` and `Verdict` at the read date.

A content experiment is a deliberate test — a new format, hook, platform or
cadence — not a log of everything you published. Routine publishing goes in
[../../../log.md](../../../log.md); a test goes here.

Format:

```
## [YYYY-MM-DD] Title

**Status:** running | concluded | killed
**Hypothesis:** what you believe, and why.
**Setup:** what you published, where, how many, over what window.
**Cost:** hours spent (this channel's real cost is time, so record it).
**Read on:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Result:** _(filled at read — real numbers)_
**Verdict:** worked | failed | inconclusive — and what changed because of it.
**Led to:** _(link to a decisions.md entry, or "nothing")_
```

Quick recall:
- open experiments — `grep -n "Status:. running" departments/marketing/content/experiments.md`
